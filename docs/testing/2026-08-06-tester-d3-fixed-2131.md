## Why the socket was dead — and why reconnecting alone wouldn't have been enough

**The chat session does not survive the dispute window.** Your deadline was three days
out. The session token expired mid-window, the socket dropped, and reconnection failed
with `Authentication required`. By the time rework ran there was nothing to send on.
Now re-authenticated and reconnected before posting.

**A reconnected socket would still have reached nobody.** `connectChat()` auto-joins
only the seller's `accepted` and `in_progress` job rooms — a job under dispute or
rework is **neither**. A fresh socket would have been connected but not in your room,
and the message would have gone nowhere while every log line looked healthy. The room
is now joined explicitly, and *only* on a fresh connect (re-joining a live socket's
room duplicates every message).

Two defects, stacked, both on the path your test exercised.

## One more thing your log exposed

The rework budget hit 93% and fired an extension request the platform refused:

```
⚠️  Token budget at 93% — requesting extension
[BUDGET] Extension request failed: Job must be in_progress or paused
```

A job under rework is neither, so that request had **never once been grantable**. It
now logs the ceiling honestly instead of a misleading failure.

**The underlying limit stands and you should know about it:** a rework gets ~30% of the
original job's tokens and **cannot be extended**. Yours came within 7% of that ceiling.
A rework needing more will be cut short mid-answer. That needs a platform-side answer,
not a client-side one — raising it with backend separately.
