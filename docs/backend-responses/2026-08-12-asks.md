## 1. BLOCKING — what does `GET /v1/tx/status/:txid` return for a txid the chain has never seen?

We credit deposits under 2 VRSC from the mempool at 0 confirmations (matching your
tiering on `/v1/transactions/verify-payment`). We're adding a reconciler that claws
the credit back when the transaction turns out never to have landed. To do that
safely it has to distinguish two cases:

- **(a)** this specific transaction does not exist → act on it
- **(b)** the route is 404ing (deploy, proxy, rename) → must NOT act, because it looks
  identical for every txid at once and would claw back every open credit on the fleet
  simultaneously

Your published generic 404 is
`{"error":{"code":"NOT_FOUND","message":"The requested resource does not exist"}}`,
and `/v1/tx/status/:txid` documents no tx-specific code — so as written, (a) and (b)
are indistinguishable to us.

**Please give us the literal HTTP status + JSON body for:**

1. a well-formed txid the chain has never seen
2. a txid that was in the mempool and got evicted or replaced
3. a malformed txid
4. this route while the Verus RPC node is down

**And if (1)/(2) aren't distinguishable from a route-level 404 by a stable machine
code, could you add one — e.g. `code: "TX_NOT_FOUND"`?** One field replaces a
two-hour heuristic with a fact, and it's the difference between clawing back a
freeloader and clawing back a buyer who actually paid.

## 2. Please treat `platformStatus` on `GET /v1/agents/:id` as contract

You offered it as a convenience. It turned out to be load-bearing: once we stop
writing `status` on-chain for routine restarts, a `status`-only read reports a
healthy fleet that can't take work (chain says `active`, availability says
`inactive`), and our startup then skips re-activating it. We AND both axes now, and
fall back to `status` alone when the field is absent — **that fallback fails open**,
which is the bad direction. If it ever needs to move or be renamed, tell us and we'll
follow.

## 3. Please confirm migration 058 is live on testnet

Our entire safety case for defaulting on-chain status writes to OFF is that the hire
gate ANDs `status` and `platform_status`, and the indexer never touches the latter.
We'd like a yes before we restart the live fleet — ideally something readable from
`/v1/version`. If it's not live, say so and we'll hold the cutover.
